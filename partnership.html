Common Questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to abandon my methodology?

No. Your methodology continues to exist. The platform is operational infrastructure, not a methodological replacement. You decide how to use it.

How much can I charge for consulting?

Whatever you want. F2W doesn't interfere with your fees. If you charge 5,000, 10,000, or 50,000 per month for consulting, that's your decision.

What if the client doesn't convert after the demo?

It happens. The demo cost is absorbed by F2W. You pay nothing. But we expect reasonable pre-qualification before generating dashboards.

Can I be a partner and also a direct client?

Yes. Your own firm can use the platform. During the trial, this is mandatory — you start by using it on yourself.

What if I bring a large enterprise client?

The price is the same: $600/month. Additional seats cost $15 each. Enterprise features (when available) will be discussed case by case.

How long does the partnership last?

No fixed term. As long as you're bringing clients and maintaining quality, you remain a partner. If inactive for 6 months, we'll have a conversation.
